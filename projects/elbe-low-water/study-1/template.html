Legible only below

A study. The dates carved on three Elbe stones, readable only while the river reads low. Move through the month.

    %%STONES%%

The rule is this study's own: a stone's dates are legible while the Dresden gauge reads below its mean low water (%%MNW%% cm). It does not claim any stone is exposed at that level; the stones' own thresholds are known for two only (Děčín, a discharge; Schönebeck, a level) and are not used here. Without scripts this page shows the dates legible, which is the state of %%BELOW%% % of the %%N%% readings held.

The record. Lowest held: %%LO%% cm at %%LOT%% (Europe/Berlin). Stones' dates: the Saxon state table “Hungersteine und Untiefen der Elbe” (LHWZ Sachsen / Senckenberg Dresden, status 24.07.2018), dates only.
