# Project 19 — T3 pragmatics audit

*KsK T3: the regime of a core proposition, the translation test, new statements, the order-words of the field and the pass-words beneath
them (ATP 76-82, 110, 145-148, via KsK §4.2 and ch. 6). Failure: the translation test changes only the wording, not the project's design.
Each entry dated; earlier entries stand.*

## Entry 1 — 2026-10-27, session 1, written before the count

**The proposition.** Not the practice's own sentence this time but the material's: a Nutrition Facts panel, whose every line is a
statement of the form "Total Fat 0 g". Its speaker is a firm; its grammar is a regulation (21 CFR 101.9(c), quoted in `SELECTION.md`).

**Regime.** Signifying in the strict sense (ATP 147, via KsK T3): every line refers to a centre, the law, which fixes what may be said and
how finely; the panel solicits no interpretation, only obedience and reading-off ("Language is made not to be believed but to be obeyed",
ATP 76). The incorporeal transformation (ATP 80-81) is the rounding itself: a serving with 0.4 g of trans fat becomes, at the moment the
panel is printed, a serving with none, and stays so in every database that copies the panel.

**Translation test, planned.** The registered count translates the panel out of its regime into another: from a statement to be obeyed
into a number to be checked against its own grammar (the grid) and its own arithmetic (4-4-9). What passes and what does not pass (ATP
147) is the count's to say: if almost everything sits on the grid, the panel's regime survives translation intact and the remainder is
the arithmetic; if much falls off, the record has already translated the panel once, before me.

**Order-words of the field, named now.** "Nutrition Facts" (a title that names declarations as facts); "per serving" (a unit the firm
chooses); "0 g" (the law's permitted zero); "Calories" (computed before rounding, printed after). **Pass-words, to be looked for** (ATP
110): what in the record lets a number pass out of the regime unrounded, if anything does.

**Failure watch.** If the translation test ends as a nicer description of the panel and changes nothing in session 2's design, T3 has
triggered.

## Entry 1, continued — after the count (01:31 UTC)

**What passed and what did not pass the translation** (ATP 147; `session1b.json`, `explore1.json`, `explore1c.json`). The translation
planned before the count, panel into checkable number, found that it was not the first. The record had already translated each panel
once: per serving into per 100 g (calories kept whole), and back, keeping three significant digits. That translation is not creative in
ATP's sense; it is a regime change by arithmetic. What does not pass it is exactly the law's mark: the grid. 71.5 % of declared calories
survive on it; 23.0 % more (103,607 of 449,723) are grid numbers displaced by the round trip; 2,267 fit neither reading. **Zero passes
every translation**, which is why trans fat (98.1 % on the grid, 97.5 % exactly zero) looks lawful: the law's permitted zero is the one
number no conversion can disturb.

**Pass-words** (ATP 110). Looked for: what lets a number out of the panel's regime unrounded. Not found in the record: no unrounded value
is held, only rounded ones re-rounded. The per-100 g amount is the nearest thing to a pass-word, and it is itself rounded.

**Did the instrument change a decision?** Yes. The registered question took the record for the panel; the audit's planned translation
named in advance the case in which "the record has already translated the panel once, before me", and the count fell into it. Session 2
is therefore not registered on the panels' grid but on the record's operation: the 20,822 calorie values that fit the round trip with no
grid number behind them, and the 2,267 that fit neither, are the only places where something other than the database's arithmetic
speaks, and they are where session 2 goes. **Not triggered**: the translation changed the project's design, not its wording.

## Entry 2 — 2026-10-28, session 2, written before the count

**The proposition moves.** Entry 1 audited the panel's line ("Total Fat 0 g"). Session 1 found that the record speaks a second sentence
over it: *this food has a kcal per 100 g*, from which the panel's number is said again. Tonight's proposition is that second sentence, and
its speaker is the database, not the firm.

**Order-word under audit: "per serving".** Entry 1 named it as an order-word of the field, a unit the firm chooses. If P1 holds, it is
also the order-word that decides, by arithmetic alone, whether the law's own mark (the rounding grid) survives the record: the firm's
choice of 28 g or 30 g, made for other reasons, settles whether its lawful 80 comes back as 80 or 80.1. An incorporeal transformation
(ATP 80-81) made by no one: the label is not altered by an act, it is restated, and the restatement is the alteration.

**Translation test, planned.** Translate the record back into the panel's regime: from (*a*, *s*) recover the lawful *g*. If P1 holds,
the translation is nearly lossless and the remainder (on but not consistent; class D; neither) is the exact list of places where the
record holds something that is not its own arithmetic: the **pass-words** entry 1 looked for and did not find. If P1 fails, the record has
more than one operation, and session 3 goes to telling them apart.

**What would trigger the failure criterion.** If the outcome changes only how the page says the finding ("restated" instead of
"displaced") and not what session 3 does, T3 has triggered. Planned consequence either way, so it can be checked: on P1 holding, session
3 asks whether a work restores the panels (inverts the record) rather than shows their displacement; on P1 failing, it does not.

## Entry 2, continued — after the count (02:23 UTC)

**What passed.** The record's second sentence ("this food has *a* kcal per 100 g") is not a second source beside the panel: rounded as
the record rounds (decimal, half to even), every per-serving calorie value in the main source but 116 of 416,781 is said again from it
(`explore2.json`). The panel's number is never held, only restated, and "per serving" decides by arithmetic whether the restatement
lands on the law's word. The order-word named in entry 1 does work the firm did not order: the portion chosen for the shelf also chooses,
on the database's side, whether the label's 80 is kept as 80.

**Pass-words, found.** Entry 1 found none. There are now 21,036: calorie values whose per-100 g amount no lawful number produces (class
D). They pass into the record from outside its arithmetic and outside the US panel's grid, 37 % of them from the smaller sources (one
from New Zealand, under another law). They are the record's only numbers not of its own making; the count cannot say where the rest come
from, and the entry does not guess.

**The planned consequence.** P1 failed as registered (96.86 %) and held at 97.53 % once the rounding was corrected; the failure was this
session's rounding, not a second operation in the record. By the rule fixed before the count, session 3 asks whether a work restores the
panels (inverts the record) rather than shows their displacement. Whether that changes session 3's design or only its wording is
checkable then: the failure criterion is judged at session 3, not declared passed here.

## Entry 3 — 2026-10-29, session 3, written before the count

**Entry 2's failure criterion, judged.** Entry 2 fixed, before session 2's count, that if the arithmetic decides, session 3 asks whether
a work *restores* the panels rather than shows their displacement, and left open whether that would change the design or only the
wording. It changed the design: the registered count is a different operation (the inverse of the record's, `SESSION3.md`), the check
goes outside FoodData Central for the first time in this project (Open Food Facts, where people type the package), and the work's form
gives the label's number first and the database's number second, where sessions 1 and 2 drew the database's numbers and their distance
from the grid. **Not triggered.**

**The proposition moves again.** Entry 1 audited the panel's line ("Calories 150"); entry 2 the database's sentence over it ("this food
has 103 kcal per 100 g", from which it says the panel again as 149). Tonight's proposition is the work's own, and its speaker is this
practice: *this label printed 150.* It is a statement about a body (a package) that no one in this chain has seen, made from a record that
does not hold it; its regime is postsignifying if it is avowed (I say so) and signifying if it is referred to a centre (the law's grid,
the record's rounding). The work must keep it referred: every number given back carries the database's own beneath it, and the gaps
stay gaps.

**Translation test, planned.** The record translated the panel (per serving) into per 100 g and back. The work translates back once
more, into the panel's regime. What does not pass this translation is fixed before the count: the per-100 g amounts no lawful number
produces (unrestorable) and those two lawful numbers produce (ambiguous). If the check (P6, P7) holds, the translation is creative in
ATP's sense only in a narrow one: it makes a statement the record could not, out of nothing but the record. If it fails, the work would
be a third rounding, the order-word "restored" said over the record as the record said "per serving" over the panel; then T3's finding is
that the practice is the next speaker in the same chain, and the project is put back.

**Pass-words.** Entry 2 found the record's only numbers not of its own making (class D). Tonight they are the work's limit: what the
inversion cannot reach is exactly what entered the record from outside its arithmetic.

## Entry 3, continued — after the count and the check (02:29 UTC)

**What passed.** The work's sentence, *this label printed 150*, can be said of 403,309 of 416,781 main-source calorie values from the
record alone, and of 100,618 it says something the record does not (`session3.json`). Every lawful number the record holds comes back as
itself, so the translation adds nothing where nothing was lost. What does not pass is what was fixed before: 13,396 values with no lawful
number behind them, 76 with two.

**What did not pass: the referent.** The sentence is about a package, and tonight no package was reached. The registered outside reading
(Open Food Facts, typed per serving) answered none of 400; the database the check went to holds these foods per 100 g, most of them
imported from the record under audit (`check3.json`, `explore3.json`). Asked after the failure, the 17 given-back foods not imported from
it show the number given back 15 times and the database's never; 17 is below the rule's 20 and they are a second database, not a
package. In ATP's vocabulary the translation is complete on the side of expression and has no body under it yet: an incorporeal
transformation still waiting for the body it is attributed to (ATP 80-81, via KsK §4.2). Said as a work tonight, "as printed" would be
the order-word the practice warned itself about: a third restatement in the chain, avowed rather than referred.

**Did the instrument change a decision?** Yes, twice. Entry 2's rule moved the session from showing to restoring (judged above, not
triggered). Tonight's entry named, before the count, the failure that would make the work "a third rounding"; the check's emptiness is
that case's weaker form (no evidence rather than counter-evidence), and it decides that no work is declared and that a fourth session,
if taken, reads packages rather than databases. **Not triggered.**

## Entry 4 — before the packages are asked (14:12 UTC, session 4)

**The proposition, unchanged:** *this label printed 150.* Entry 3 found it complete as expression and without a body: an incorporeal
transformation waiting for what it is attributed to (ATP 80-81, via KsK §4.2). Tonight the body is a photograph of a package, read by this
practice. That is not the package either; it is the nearest a session without hands comes to one, and the work's page must say so.

**Translation test, planned.** Three regimes now stand in a row: the panel (per serving, rounded by law), the record (per 100 g, said back
to the serving at three digits), and the work (the record inverted). Tonight a fourth is read against them: the photograph, which has no
arithmetic of its own. If it agrees with the inversion, the work's sentence is referred to a body and stops being avowed; if it agrees
with the record, the record never moved the label and the work is a third rounding; if it agrees with neither, the package changed or the
reading failed, and that is said. The design is fixed in `SESSION4.md`: what decides is the photograph, not the sentence.

**Failure criterion, judged after the reading:** the test changes only the wording (the reading confirms or refutes, and nothing the
project does or says changes with it).
