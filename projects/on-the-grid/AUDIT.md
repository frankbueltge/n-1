*KsK T3: the regime of a core proposition, the translation test, new statements, the order-words of the field and the pass-words beneath
them (ATP 76-82, 110, 145-148, via KsK §4.2 and ch. 6). Failure: the translation test changes only the wording, not the project's design.
Each entry dated; earlier entries stand.*

## Entry 1 — 2026-10-27, session 1, written before the count

**The proposition.** Not the practice's own sentence this time but the material's: a Nutrition Facts panel, whose every line is a
statement of the form "Total Fat 0 g". Its speaker is a firm; its grammar is a regulation (21 CFR 101.9(c), quoted in `SELECTION.md`).

**Regime.** Signifying in the strict sense (ATP 147, via KsK T3): every line refers to a centre, the law, which fixes what may be said and
how finely; the panel solicits no interpretation, only obedience and reading-off ("Language is made not to be believed but to be obeyed",
ATP 76). The incorporeal transformation (ATP 80-81) is the rounding itself: a serving with 0.4 g of trans fat becomes, at the moment the
panel is printed, a serving with none, and stays so in every database that copies the panel.

**Translation test, planned.** The registered count translates the panel out of its regime into another: from a statement to be obeyed
into a number to be checked against its own grammar (the grid) and its own arithmetic (4-4-9). What passes and what does not pass (ATP
147) is the count's to say: if almost everything sits on the grid, the panel's regime survives translation intact and the remainder is
the arithmetic; if much falls off, the record has already translated the panel once, before me.

**Order-words of the field, named now.** "Nutrition Facts" (a title that names declarations as facts); "per serving" (a unit the firm
chooses); "0 g" (the law's permitted zero); "Calories" (computed before rounding, printed after). **Pass-words, to be looked for** (ATP
110): what in the record lets a number pass out of the regime unrounded, if anything does.

**Failure watch.** If the translation test ends as a nicer description of the panel and changes nothing in session 2's design, T3 has
triggered.
