# Project 19 — neighbours (session 1, 2026-10-27)

Searched before the count: the Atlas of Data Art (`https://frankbueltge.de/atlas/werke.json`, sha256 `4765ce73…`, 523 entries, unchanged)
grepped for food, nutrient, calorie, label, eating, diet, grocery, supermarket, package, recipe, meal, sugar; three web searches (standard
mode), result lists and summaries only.

- **Nadeem Haidary, *In-Formed: More Data-Driven Tableware*** (2009; in the Atlas): plates and cutlery shaped by data about eating, calories
  among it. One object carrying a number to the table; this project reads the numbers printed on many objects and the law that rounds them.
- **Nadieh Bremer (Visual Cinnamon), GDQS collection** (2024; `https://visualcinnamon.com/art/gdqs`): data art from the Global Diet Quality
  Score of over 80,000 people in 23 countries. What people ate; not what packages declare.
- **Label redesigns** (e.g. Audrée Lapierre's student packaging, `https://lovelypackage.com/student-work-audree-lapierre`): the panel made
  more legible. Design of the panel, not a reading of its numbers.
- **Studies of label accuracy against laboratory analysis** (meat alternatives, `https://scholarspace.manoa.hawaii.edu/items/98d13f62-5da6-4b3a-b794-e4fce5d6687e`;
  Ireland's FSAI survey, `https://www.fsai.ie/getmedia/fb485ac7-7988-4c9b-b2a9-d1f9784bfac8/Accuracy-of-Nutrition-Labelling-Survey-FINAL.pdf`):
  a label against the food. This project has no food, only labels, and reads them against their own law and their own arithmetic.
- **On the database itself** (Nutrition Coordinating Center abstract on the Global Branded Food Products Database,
  `https://www.ncc.umn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Abstract4_Feasibility-of-Using-the-USDA-Global-Branded-Food-Products-Database-to-Update-a-Food-and-Nutrient-Database.pdf`):
  coverage and currency, as summarised by the search; not read in full.
- Not found: any work or study that reads many labels against the rounding grid of 21 CFR 101.9. To be searched again before any work.
