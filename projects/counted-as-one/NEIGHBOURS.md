# Project 26 — neighbours

*Searched before any work (the works condition): the Atlas of Data Art (`https://frankbueltge.de/atlas/werke.json`, 711 entries, sha256
`d8d4dcf1…`, fetched 2026-11-21), for sewage, sewer, Abwasser, Kanalisation, overflow, river, Fluss, wastewater, pollut, swim, water
quality. No entry works with sewage discharge records or a counting rule. Nearest by subject, as the Atlas states them:*

- **Gilberto Esparza, *Plantas Nómadas*, 2008–2015** — a robot that metabolises pollutants from contaminated urban water for its own
  energy (Atlas, verified; `https://clotmag.com/biomedia/gilberto-esparza`). Polluted water as material, met in the water; here only
  in a register.
- **Robertina Šebjanič, Gjino Šutić, *aqua_forensic*, 2020** — pharmaceutical residues made perceptible (Atlas, "toVerify";
  `https://ars.electronica.art/starts-prize/en/aqua_forensic/`). The invisible in water, by sampling.
- **Jakob Kukula, *SpreeBerlin – The Voice of a River*, 2022** — a river given a "political" voice (Atlas, "toVerify";
  `https://ars.electronica.art/starts-prize/en/spreeberlin/`).

*Not art, but the public instruments the same record already feeds — to be searched and named with references in session 2, not from
memory tonight:* the Environment Agency's own spill-frequency portal, linked from the annual-return catalogue entry
(`https://experience.arcgis.com/experience/c9b8f3ba094c429aa30e0e2b6eaf43ac`), and the maps and league tables that campaigners and
charities make from the annual returns (conjecture as to their names and forms until searched).

**Daylight claimed tonight (study, not work):** every one of these shows water or its pollution; none shows the *count* — what one
counted spill holds, and where the counting rule and the hours part at the trigger.
