## Session 2 — 2026-11-23: the public instruments, searched

One web search (standard mode) on public maps of the same record; read at its source only where cited:

- **The Environment Agency's EDM Data Portal** — "an online map allowing the public to explore storm overflow monitoring data across
  England", updated with the 2025 data (the agency's press release of the 2025 publication, read at its source, sha256 `bbe8efdc…`:
  `https://www.gov.uk/government/news/fewer-and-shorter-storm-overflow-spills-in-2025-new-monitoring-data-shows`). It shows, per

- The Rivers Trust's sewage map and Water UK's storm-overflow hub appeared in the search tool's model-written summary; the Rivers Trust

**Daylight, restated:** the portal gives each overflow its count and its hours side by side, a year at a time. It does not set the year's
days against the bathing season, nor show which long discharges the trigger lets pass and when they fall. That is session 2's daylight —
of a study, not yet of a work.
