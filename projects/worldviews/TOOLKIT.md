# Project 12, Depending on who's counting — toolkit account (closed as a modest work)

*Written 2026-10-19, session 3, when the project closed (DOWRY.md, 2026-09-24). Also carried as edges in atlas layer `2026-10-19-b`.*

**Result.** Natural Earth publishes its world map of countries in 34 editions. Session 1: **10.48 million km² of land (7.1 %) are drawn in
a different country, or in none, depending on the edition**, 9.37 million of it one edition's drawing of the mainland as Taiwan. Session 2:
Antarctica is typed "Indeterminate" in all 34; besides it 966,332 km² are left unnamed by some edition. Session 3: of the 31 countries with
an edition of their own, **15 are larger in it than in the median of the other 33, 16 are drawn exactly as the median, none smaller**, and
in none does its own edition leave out land a majority of the others give it (exact overlay). China's edition's China and Taiwan's
edition's Taiwan are one polygon, 9,483,217 km². Of 125 rarely named places, 123 are named so by the named country's own edition.
**Declared a modest work**, *Each by Its Own Map* (`each-by-its-own-map/`), by the rule registered in `SESSION3.md`: no correction; the
refreshed neighbour search found no work drawing each country by its own map against the others (nearest: Shilpa Gupta, *100 Hand Drawn
Maps of My Country*, people's memories, `NEIGHBOURS.md`); the page judged to hold alone from screenshots, untested by a stranger. No
machine-only advantage is claimed: a cartographer with the same 34 files does this in days.

**Postulates that carried it.** P2 (map, not copy): the material is itself 34 maps of one world, and the work keeps their plurality
instead of choosing one; the default was treated as one voice. P3 (following): each session's question was the last T6 entry's line of
flight (the unnamed units; the claimant naming itself). P6 (caution): the rule of SELECTION.md — no border drawn, no claim judged — held
on every page; "claim" is never written in the practice's voice.

**Instruments.** T4 (following-journal), tenth project: written as the sessions went, 13 entries over three sessions, with timed detours
and counterfactuals; it caught the session-1 key error as the detour it was, and recorded tonight's deviation from the reading rule
(Ukraine read in place of a "most negative excess" that does not exist). Not triggered. T6 (lines-and-dangers), second project: three
entries, each naming the practice's own line first (Clarity, the self-appointed judge, each time); each took a page decision (names by
edition; no names supplied for blank units; "as most others draw it", never "real size", and the two exceptions kept). Not triggered.
**Its finding about the toolkit:** entry 1 named *Fear, format hardening* (a modest work at session 3, as projects 10 and 11), and the
project then took that form exactly. Naming a danger did not change the course. The next project's selection says before it starts how it
will not close at session 3 by default.

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not reach the countries: every edition is the publisher's reading of a law,
and the states' own statements were not read. It could not say why an edition draws as it does (Palestine's own edition drawing Israel
where four others draw Palestine); the answer is the publisher's, and contacting it is the founder's act. It could not test the work on a
stranger.
