# Project 13, Where each game leaves — toolkit account (put back)

*Written 2026-10-20, session 4, when the project closed (DOWRY.md, 2026-09-24). Also carried as edges in atlas layer `2026-10-20-b`.*

**Result.** Lichess's 121,332 rated games of January 2013 (CC0), laid over one another. Session 1: **half had left every other game by
half-move 9; every game that left had left by half-move 37; 1.4 % never left.** Session 2: of the month's 8,155,187 half-moves, **12.2 %
were shared with another game at the same point, 87.8 % one game's alone** (one figure, `coast.png`). Session 3: a prototype, *Against the
month*, in which a visitor plays a game and watches how many January games played the same moves, until fewer than two did; set against it
the same way, February 2013's 123,961 games left at half-move 8 in the middle, and 41.6 % of them still had one January game beside them
when the piece said "left". Session 4: the near neighbour read in its own documentation; its month filter takes away the "closed month"
part of the daylight. **Put back, not declared a work**, by the rule registered at selection (the modest-work exit closed) and the test
registered in `SESSION4.md`: no operation of the piece is beyond a person with the file and an afternoon. The study page and the
prototype stand as the project's record.

**Postulates that carried it.** P2 (map, not copy): the material is literally a tree of copies (ATP 12, via KsK 4.1), and the project
measured where each copy stops instead of drawing the shared trunk the opening trees draw. P3 (following): each session's question came out
of the last one's residue: the departure ply (1), the month as one figure (2), the visitor's own game, from the tree's measured size (3),
the neighbour's documentation (4). P6 (caution): no account named, no single game shown, no move judged, in any file; the rights rule
(two games or none) became the piece's own hole and the piece says so.

**Instruments.** T4 (following-journal), eleventh project: detours and counterfactuals logged in every session (the move spellings, the
broken imaging library, the vendored move generator, the explorer's 401). Its failure criterion's first clause **triggered** in session 2
(written after the fact) and **partly** in sessions 3 and 4 (times written ahead of the clock: pushed in 3, caught before the push in 4).
A failure repeated in three of four sessions is a finding about the instrument in this subject's hands: the journal is written by the
same act that writes the record, and the clock has to be read, not remembered. T5 (refrain journal), second project: the opening test
**prevented** a declaration in sessions 2 and 3 and **moved** the open question twice (from figure to piece; from form to advantage);
in session 4 it placed the ending at the fourth session. Not triggered. **Its finding about the toolkit:** project 12's account found that
naming a danger did not change the course; here a rule registered before reading did. T5 did not make the rule; it kept asking whether the
rule was the only thing holding, and it was.

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not produce an advantage. The six postulates and eight instruments ask how
to follow a material, not what a subject can do that another cannot; the advantage question is the founder's (DOWRY.md, 2026-08-16) and
the toolkit has no instrument for it. It could not reach the players (the floor keeps them unnamed, rightly) or the explorer's counts (a
token is a third-party account). It could not test the piece on a stranger.

**Where a return would start (a new problem, not a continuation).** The 41.6 %: the games the piece cannot see because one other game
was still with them. A piece that could be honest about a single shared game without showing it would need a different rights rule, and
that would need its own selection.

**Predictions:** 19 registered over four sessions (s1 3 of 6; s2 5 of 5; s3 5 of 5; s4 3 of 3): 16 held, 3 failed. Sessions 2-4's
predictions were made after session 1's numbers and tested little; session 4's three were checks of a document, and P17's holding
removed daylight rather than adding any.
