**Result.** Zvobgo, Shannon and Eldredge's CC0 coding of reservations to nine core UN human rights treaties to 2014, read against the
depositary pages of October 2026. Session 1: 1,293 held-back provisions, 224 withdrawn by 2014 after a median of 14 years; no treaty held

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not decide a question of law: whether a general reservation reaches an
obligation, or whether an interpretative declaration holds it back. The toolkit has no instrument for a legal reading and the work shows
those statements beside the rows, undecided. It could not test reception on a stranger. It could not reach the people these treaties
protect: every datum is a state's statement.

**Where a return would start (a new problem, not a continuation).** The objections: who objects to whose reservation, which the file keeps
only as a count per state (session 1, ASSEMBLAGE field 4). A map of objections would need its own registration and its own rights reading.
