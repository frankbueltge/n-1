# Project 15 — T2 assemblage analysis

*KsK T2: four fields, bodies, statements, territorialisations, cutting edges (ATP 88, 503-505); "neither base nor superstructure"
(ATP 90). Failure: field 4 stays empty or without consequence, or the fields are filled without a movement between them.*

## Session 1 — 2026-10-22

- **Field 1, bodies.** States (185 parties to CEDAW in the file, 43 to CED); the Secretary-General as depositary; the pages and their
  footnotes; the scholars' file; this practice reading both.
- **Field 2, statements.** Ratification (a state becomes a party, dated), reservation and declaration (a part excepted, in the same
  act), objection (another state's statement about the first), withdrawal (the exception ended, dated to the day on the page, to the
  year in the file). Each is an incorporeal transformation in the foundation's sense (ATP 80-81, via KsK §4.2): nothing in the bodies
  changes when 16 (1) is held back; what the state is bound by does.
- **Field 3, territorialisations.** What the statements name as their ground: the Family Code, the Sharia, a Constitution, an
  electoral law, the licensing of broadcasting, Jersey (all read tonight in the check, `readings.json`).
- **Field 4, cutting edges.** The withdrawal: 224 of 1,293 held-back provisions returned by 2014, after a median of 14 years. And a
  second edge found tonight: the objection, a statement by one state that tries to unmake another's exception (the Czech Republic's objection of 2016 to
  Somalia's reservation to the CRC holds the Convention in force between them without the reservation; read under its entry). The file counts objections only as
  a number per state (`objections`), not who objected to what.

**Movement between fields.** A territorialisation (field 3, a domestic law) is changed, and a statement (field 2, a withdrawal) follows:
the Irish footnote on CEDAW ties withdrawals of 1986 to two Acts passed that year, and the Austrian one ties its last withdrawal (2015)
to a review of the reservation (paraphrased; the wording is not quoted, SELECTION.md, rights). Whether that order holds generally is a question, not a finding.

**Did it touch a decision?** Partly: field 4's second edge (objection) is the movement the file flattens to a count, and it joins the
audit's list for session 2. Not triggered: field 4 holds two edges with consequence for the next registration.
