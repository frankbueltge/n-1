# Project 21 — assemblage analysis (T2)

*One entry per session. The four fields are vectors, not drawers (KsK T2); each entry ends with the movement between them it found, or
says it found none.*

## Entry 1 — session 1, 2026-11-01

- **Field 1, bodies.** A slab of concrete or asphalt, kilometres long, fixed to the crust; paint on it; aircraft and their compasses; the
  outer core's flow, which makes a field at the surface that turns, at a runway in Alaska, by more than a degree in five years
  (`explore1.json`, examples). The bodies the record cannot hold: the paint itself.
- **Field 2, statements.** The number. It is a statement about a body (this slab points 087° magnetic) that becomes false without the body
  changing, because the reference it is made against moves. The renumbering is an incorporeal transformation in ATP's sense (80-81, via
  KsK §4.2): by notice and paint, on a datable night, the runway becomes another runway. Second statement: OurAirports' row, which repeats
  the number at a further remove and can lag the paint.
- **Field 3, territorialities.** The rule (magnetic heading / 10, rounded); the regulators who decide when a number is wrong enough to
  change (the thresholds the news pieces disagree on: 3° or 7°, `NEIGHBOURS.md`); the charts; the crowd that keeps the database. And one
  territory that refuses the moving reference: Canada's northern airspace, numbered by true north (`session1.json`, 23 of 26 T runways).
- **Field 4, cutting edge.** The field itself, which deterritorializes every number at a rate set by the planet and not by anyone at the
  airport. It is not a metaphor here: it is the variable the count turns on, and where it is fast (≥ 0.2°/yr) the out-of-step share is
  45 % against 18 % where it is still (`session1.json`, P6).
- **Movement found.** Field 4 acts on field 2 directly, through no body: the statement decays while the bodies stand. Field 3 answers
  it, late, by a new statement (renumbering), and the lag between the two is measurable: 93 % of out-of-step numbers stand off on the side
  the field moved away from (`explore1.json`), and the numbers fit the field of 2002 best (`explore1b.json`). That lag — a statement
  outlived by its reference — is the problem the next sessions follow. Field 4 not empty; not triggered.
