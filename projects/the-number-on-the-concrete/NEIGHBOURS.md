# Project 21 — nearest neighbours (searched before any work, 2026-11-01)

*What the world already holds. Two web searches (standard mode, 02:13 UTC), result lists and summaries only — nothing below is quoted from
a page read in full; the Atlas of Data Art (`werke.json`, sha256 `4765ce73…`, 523 entries) grepped for runway, airport, magnet, compass,
declination, aviation, flight.*

1. **The renumbering as news.** Single airports renumbered when the field moved: Tampa International, 2011 (Popular Science,
   `https://www.popsci.com/technology/article/2011-01/shifting-magnetic-north-forces-tampa-airport-close-runways-repaint-heading-designations`);
   Cork Airport, 17/35 to 16/34, "for the first time in 50 years" (Irish Examiner,
   `https://www.irishexaminer.com/ireland/designation-numbers-of-cork-airport-runway-to-change-for-first-time-50-years-377269.html`);
   Fairbanks and Princess Juliana named in explainer pieces (e.g. Flying, `https://flyingmag.com/why-do-runways-get-renumbered`). Each is
   one runway at the moment it is put right. **Daylight:** none of them counts the runways not yet put right, or reads a date out of the
   whole. The search summary reported no map or dataset of every airport's renumbering status (search summary, conjecture that none exists).
2. **Anna Rún Tryggvadóttir, *Multipolar*** (Listasafn Íslands, National Gallery of Iceland; `https://listasafn.is/en/art/exhibitions/multipolar`):
   the wandering magnetic pole as an exhibition, a needle "in constant unraveling" and watercolours of the poles' past and future places
   (from the search summary). The nearest neighbour in art. **Daylight:** it figures the field's motion; this study reads the motion out of
   twelve thousand human marks that did not move with it.
3. **"View From 30,000 Feet"** (MetaFilter Projects, `https://projects.metafilter.com/5357/View-From-30000-Feet`): minimalist drawings of
   airports' runways generated from coordinates, angles and dimensions, the maker noting the bearings are magnetic (search summary). The
   nearest in material and in form. **Daylight:** it draws runways as they stand; it does not set them against the field of any year.
4. **The Atlas of Data Art:** no work on runways or the geomagnetic field. Three hits on the grep (Sakamoto and Manabe, *Sensing Streams*,
   invisible electromagnetic flows; Keysar and Caine, *Terra Ex Machina*; Bellingcat, *MH17*), none near.
