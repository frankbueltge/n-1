# Earth's rotation — assemblage analysis (T2)

*The project's second instrument (KsK ch. 6, T2), used here for the first time
in this practice. Begun in session 1, 2026-09-25, after the first prospect
had read the material (`material/earth-rotation/2026-09-25-prospect/`). The

between them. Revised by dated additions only.*

## Session 1 — first pass

**Field 1: bodies.** A planet whose rotation varies by milliseconds a day with
tides, core, oceans and ice (the CCTF report sorts these into modelled and
stochastic causes, §4). Radio telescopes that measure the rotation against
quasars, and Paris Observatory, which reduces the measurements into one series
of 23,613 days. Hundreds of atomic clocks averaged into TAI. The servers,
satellites, grids and telephone networks whose software counts seconds, and
which the draft resolution describes as "largely unprepared" for a negative
leap second.

**Field 2: statements.** Bulletin C, twice a year, "To authorities responsible
for the measurement and distribution of time". Each one is an incorporeal
transformation in the foundation's exact sense, "datable to the hour, minute,
and second" (ATP 81, via KsK §4.2): a positive bulletin makes one minute in
the world's clocks sixty-one seconds long, and nothing in any body changes
when that happens. Beside it: the leap-second table with an expiry date; the
2022 resolution; Draft Resolution C, which would make the statement itself
impossible from 20 May 2027 on.

**Field 3: territorializations.** UTC as "the basis of civil time in most
countries" (Draft Resolution C, recalling 2018 Resolution 2). The ±0.9 s
tolerance, which kept the atomic clock *on* the planet's rotation for 54
years. The six-monthly rhythm of the bulletin, a refrain in the plain sense:
since 2017 it has been held by repeating "NO" twenty times.

**Field 4: cutting edges of deterritorialization.** Civil time leaving the
planet. With |UT1−UTC| allowed to reach an hour, the Earth's rotation stops
entering the world's clocks, possibly for centuries: the report weighs one
hour as "about a millennium". The rotation goes on being measured; it simply
stops being obeyed. The cutting edge runs *through* field 2. The last kind of
statement that let the planet's body act on the clocks is abolished by a
statement.

**Movements between the fields, the part that makes this analysis more than
filling in forms:**

- *Body → statement → body.* Shorter days (field 1) raise UT1−UTC. A
  bulletin (field 2) would then have to take a second *out* of every clock
  (field 1 again), which has never been done. The statement that the bodies'
  own motion calls for is the one the machine bodies are said to be unable to
  bear.
- *Anticipated body → statement.* The decision rests on a probability
  (a negative leap second, "∼ 30 %" within ten years) of an event that has
  never occurred. What is being prevented is a statement that was never made.
- *Territory held by coincidence.* At the moment the coupling is to be cut,
  the two times agree to within 0.0071 s (2026-08-25). They are not drifting
  apart. The coupling is cut while it is closest to closed.

## What T2 has done so far

It separated what the prospect had read as one story into two segments,
bodies and statements, and put the cutting edge where they meet. The
decision it touched is in `JOURNAL.md`, session 1. Failure criterion
(field 4 empty or without consequence; no movement described): **not
triggered at this pass.** Field 4 is filled, and it has consequences only
if later sessions work on it; that is what the criterion will check at the
close.
