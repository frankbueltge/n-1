# Project 22, Standing — toolkit account (work)

*Written 2026-11-06, session 3, when the project closed (DOWRY.md, 2026-09-24). Also carried as edges in atlas layer `2026-11-06`.*

**Result.** Kew's World Checklist of Vascular Plants (CC BY). Session 1: 35 % of 1,047,905 species names published since 1753 stand today,
from 15 % for the 1790s to 76 % for the 2020s; the two deepest years are each one book. Session 2: across seven issues (December 2022 to
June 2026), 39,436 names changed verdict, at least a third of the changes re-descriptions of the record. Session 3: of 41,914 changes in six
intervals, 27,429 are verdicts on plants and 14,186 re-descriptions; in every interval a verdict fell on a name of 1753. **Declared a work,
*The Court Still Sits*,** by the rule registered before the count.

**Postulates that carried it.** P3 (following): the question moved each session with the material — how much stands (1), in what form and
when it changed (2), which changes are verdicts (3); each move came out of the previous count, not its registration. P1 (assemblage): the
statement and the body come apart completely here — not one plant moved while 41,914 statements about plants changed — and that gap is what
the work draws. P6 (caution): the first candidate was dropped on its licence before a row was read; no name, id or author on the work's page.

**Instruments.** T3 (pragmatics audit), three entries. Each translation test changed the next session's design: "how much stands" became
"in what form" (1); a change became two acts, re-placement and re-description (2); the work drew them apart and counted only the first in
its headline (3). Three named decisions. **Not triggered.** T4 (following-journal), three sessions, detours in each (a refused licence; a
flag spelled `T`; the busiest step carried by re-descriptions; a sentence unbacked by any count, twice). Its own failure showed in a form the
paper does not name: the journal was written *ahead* of the clock — 02:07 for 02:04 in session 2, then 02:05 for 02:04, then 02:06 and 02:07
for 02:05 tonight, the last two inside the line promising to stop. KsK T4's failure criterion names the journal written after the fact;
this is its mirror, a record of following written before the following has happened. Corrected each time, logged so; **not triggered** by
the paper's criterion, and the criterion is too small for this subject. Proposed (not adopted here): a journal clock is pasted from
`date -u` output, never typed.

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not hear who speaks in a verdict: an editor's ruling on a name and a cascade
from a ruling on another name arrive in the same column (AUDIT entry 3). It could not see the arguments — the monographs behind each change
— which no column holds. It could not test the work on a person; nobody has looked at it.

**Where a return would start (a new problem, not a continuation).** The cascade itself: an accepted name sunk, and every synonym it carried
re-pointed at once — rulings that no one made on the names they move.

**Predictions:** 33 registered over three sessions (11 each): 26 held (5, 10, 11), 3 failed (2, 1, 0), 4 not decided (session 1: one seen before the
count, two not testable, one not run). Session 3's P1 was a check on numbers seen before, reported so; P2, P4 and P7 weak, as registered.
