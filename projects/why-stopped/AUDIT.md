# Project 16 — T3 pragmatics audit, turned on the material

*First entry, session 1, 2026-10-22, written after the registered count. KsK T3; failure: the translation test changes only wording,
not design.*

**The proposition audited** is the register's, not a sponsor's: the closed list (*terminated*, *suspended*, *withdrawn*, …) and the
order-word beside it, "why stopped". The list performs an incorporeal transformation (a study becomes *terminated* on a date, ATP 80-81
via KsK §4.2); the line asks for an account of it.

**Regime.** The list is signifying: every study referred to one centre and its codes. The line receives answers in more than one
regime. Most in the check are reports (a cause, named: "lack of enrollment", "funder decision"). Some answer a question the line did
not ask: one of the forty says only that the stop was not for safety reasons, which is an avowal to a face that is not on the page,
the reader who would suspect harm. Read this way, the line is where the register's centre is answered and also where its suspicion is
pre-empted. Paraphrased, not quoted; the texts stay unpublished (`SELECTION.md`, rights).

**Translation test.** What passes when an event of the world is translated into the list? The *date* passes, but as the date of the
last participant's visit, not of the decision; the *state* passes, but *suspended* only for as long as it lasts (a study that resumed
leaves no stop in the present record: conjecture, `NEIGHBOURS.md`). What does not pass is the time of the event itself, which only the
line can carry, and carries in the sponsors' words, sometimes with a date in them (one in the check dates its halt to 18 March 2020 on
a study whose end the register puts in February 2020). What remains irreducible is the decision: the register has no field for when
someone decided.

**Consequence for the design.** The audit moved the question from "how much of the line did the pandemic take" (registered, counted)
to **the two clocks**: the date the register gives and the date the line implies. Session 2 is to be registered on that: whether the
register's own history of each entry (when the reason was first written) can be read, or, if not (project 15's probe of a history
endpoint was refused, `projects/held-back/LOG.md`), what the line's own dates say against the register's. Whether this counts as the
design changing is for session 2's registration to show; tonight the instrument is **not triggered** and not yet vindicated.

## Second entry — session 2, 2026-10-23

**Translation test, carried out.** Session 1 asked what passes when the event is translated into the list, and named the decision as
the irreducible remainder: the register has no field for when someone decided. Tonight the line was read for the clock it carries
itself. Of 2,012 reasons naming the pandemic on studies with an end, 65 name a month; in 20 of them the month named falls after the end
the register gives, and in 10 of those (read by eye) it is the month of the decision. So the remainder does pass, in a few lines, as
text the register cannot sort by: **the decision is translated into the free line, not into the list.** And in two lines the sentence
dates its own writing, which the register keeps only as the last change of the whole entry.

**The order-word's other face.** The probe shows the line is not a record that stays: of 47,429 studies carrying it, none is in a
status other than stopped. When a suspended study resumes, the line goes with the stop. The order-word "why stopped" is addressed only
to the stopped, and the answer lasts exactly as long as the incorporeal transformation it explains (ATP 80-81, via KsK §4.2). What
passes into the present record is the stop that stayed.

**Did the design change?** Yes, and it can be pointed to: session 1's audit moved the question to the two clocks (`SESSION2.md`, the
question, cites it), and tonight's design (the dating rule, the late line, the forward line) is that question's. The failure criterion
of T3 (the translation test changes only wording, not design) is **not triggered**; the instrument has now changed one design.
