**In research, the nearest:**

- *A longitudinal cohort study comparing clinical trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov that stopped during the first three years of
  the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic with trials that stopped in the three years prior*, medRxiv preprint (served at `https://covid19.bgcarlisle.com`;
  listed at `https://sciety.org/articles/activity/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353581`). Its abstract: 13,323 trials terminated, suspended or
  withdrawn December 2019 to November 2022, against 9,665 in the three years before, a 38 % increase. Its authorship is not shown on the
  page as read; that the address is B. G. Carlisle's is **conjecture** from the domain.
- TranspariMED, "Over 1,200 clinical trials worldwide have ground to a halt due to COVID-19", 2020-05-08,
  `https://www.transparimed.org/single-post/2020/05/08/discontinued-clinical-trials-covid`: of 2,522 trials stopped 1 December 2019 to
  5 May 2020, 1,099 explicitly cite COVID-19; identified manually, from a living paper updated daily.
- Asaad, Habibullah, Butler, "The Impact of COVID-19 on Clinical Trials", *Annals of Surgery* 272(3), 2020, e222-e223,
  doi:10.1097/SLA.0000000000004113: 1,052 trials suspended 1 March to 26 April 2020, at least 905 giving the pandemic as the reason.
- "The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on early termination of ophthalmology clinical trials: A cross-sectional analysis of
  ClinicalTrials.gov", `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10710109`: of 142 ophthalmology trials discontinued 2020 to mid-2022,
  34 (23.9 %) due to COVID-19.

**Daylight, as far as these show.** The neighbours count stops inside windows (weeks to three years), take the record as it stood when
they read it, and read the reason to classify a stop. This project reads the line itself, across the whole register to its processing
date: how long the event stays in it after it has passed, and the two clocks it shows when read against the register's own dates
(a reason naming COVID-19 on a study whose recorded end precedes 2020; 86 of them). A third difference, **conjecture** until tested:
the neighbours' 905 suspensions in eight weeks of 2020 against 198 suspended studies naming the pandemic in the register today
suggest that a stop which was resumed has left the present record; if so, the register's present remembers only the stops that stayed
stops, and the neighbours' snapshots hold what it no longer does.
