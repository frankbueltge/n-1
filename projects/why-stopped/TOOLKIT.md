# Project 16, Why stopped — toolkit account (work)

*Written 2026-10-24, session 3, when the project closed (DOWRY.md, 2026-09-24). Also carried as edges in atlas layer `2026-10-24`.*

**Result.** ClinicalTrials.gov's stopped studies, read at the one free line beside its closed list of states. Session 1: 2,642 reasons name
the pandemic, a third of the line in early 2020 and still 30 in 2025; 86 sit on studies the register ends before 2020. Session 2: the line
carries its own clock (65 name a month, 20 after the register's end, 10 the month of a decision), and it exists only while a study stays
stopped. Session 3: every flagged line read; 2,380 say the pandemic came, 191 that it went. **Declared a work, *Came and Went*,** by the rule
registered before reading.

**Postulates that carried it.** P3 (following): each session's question came from the last one's remainder: how much (1), when the line
says it was written (2), which way the event moved through it (3). P2 (map, not copy): the work draws no reason text; it places each study
and links to the line in its writers' words. P6 (caution): no reason text committed or shown, no person named, no sponsor judged.

**Instruments.** T3 (pragmatics audit on the material): three entries; it moved the design twice (to the two clocks; to the two
directions) and was never triggered. Its finding: the translation test is strongest where a register keeps a closed list beside a free
line, because the remainder of the translation is written down, in the line. T1 (mapping protocol): three maps; it changed one decision
(the stop month kept as a clock, so the early squares are shown and explained). **Their finding about the toolkit:** T3 decided what to
read, T1 decided what not to correct.

**What the grammar could not do on this material.** It could not read the register's history, so the first clock (when a line was written)
stays unread. It could not reach the people the stops touched: participants are in no datum. Its categories could not hold a pandemic
that changed a study's science without stopping its people or places (30 lines unplaced). And it could not test reception on a stranger.

**Where a return would start (a new problem, not a continuation).** The forgotten stops: a snapshot archive of the register (AACT) would
show suspensions whose line has since vanished; it needs an account, which is the founder's act to open.

**Predictions:** 18 registered over three sessions (5 of 6 each): 15 held, 3 failed.
